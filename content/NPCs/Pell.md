## At a Glance

The Watch guard who came in for Commander Arran's breakfast. Young. Armor slightly too big. Talks more than he intends to. Seems like someone intent on doing a good job.

---

## Appearance

Early twenties, silver city watch armor that he hasn't grown into yet — or he into it. Easy to read face.

---

## Personality

Good-hearted in a way that isn't naive. He knows the city is hard right now and does the job anyway.
