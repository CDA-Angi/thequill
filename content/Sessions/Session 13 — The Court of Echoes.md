# Session 13 — The Court of Echoes
#sessions #player-safe

**Date played:** TBD
**Location:** The Feywild — The Court of Echoes

---

Velistra led them in past walls that seemed to breathe with old sound. Each of them was offered a private room within the Court — the Chamber of Strings — and each of them, in turn, went in alone.

What passed between each of them and the Court is theirs alone to keep. They came out changed, in ways visible and otherwise — a locket here, a pendant there, something carried that hadn't been carried going in. None of them spoke much about it to each other. Some things, it seems, are not meant to be compared.

Then there was LeeLee.

She had been watching Silenus for some time — long enough to know, before he did, that his stay at the Court was ending. Lucian had played for her faithfully, and she released him from that service in front of all of them, with a single line and a stone pressed into his lute: *"Play well, bard. All the Feywild listens."* Silenus took his place at the Court without entirely understanding what he was agreeing to. He has been there, in one capacity or another, ever since.

They left the Court one member lighter than they'd arrived, each of them carrying something new, and none of them carrying quite the same thing.
